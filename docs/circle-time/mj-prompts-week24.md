## Notes for the picker

- The **globe** the class uses on Tuesday is a real classroom prop, deliberately not drawn — any
  illustrated globe would need country outlines and would drag lettering back in. The theme poster
  and the `ocean` poster carry the idea instead: horizon, waves, and far more water than land.
- On the sorting set the child's own basin is the control of error, so the twelve card subjects are
  exactly the twelve real objects on Tray 4. Pick the grid image whose object is nearest the one on
  your shelf, not the prettiest one.
- Reject any fish or whale with visible teeth, an open mouth, or an eye that reads as angry — resubmit
  with *friendly, rounded, mouth closed, no teeth* added before the locked suffix.
