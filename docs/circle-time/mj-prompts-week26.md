# Midjourney prompts — Circle Time, site Week 24 · “One Country — South Africa” (Mar 22–26)

Sheet week 26 in the principal's plan. 37 images → `public/circle-time-images/week24/`.
Runbook: `docs/circle-time/mj-prompts-README.md`. Style is LOCKED — do not paraphrase the suffix.

- **8 posters** `--ar 3:4`, saved 928×1232 · **28 cards + 1 badge** `--ar 1:1`, saved 1000×1000
- Flags on every prompt: `--raw --stylize 50 --v 8.2`
- Submit one prompt per 30 s through the authenticated `midjourney.com/imagine` tab.
- Cards are hyper-literal and singular — one object, centred, nothing else in frame.
- **🚨 There is NO national flag anywhere in this set.** Midjourney garbles real flags into
  six-colour mush, and a garbled national flag on a classroom wall is worse than no picture at all.
  The **real South African flag stays a Magic Box object** — a cloth flag the teacher brings in, and
  the tray-1 template the teacher draws by hand from it. Every `flag` image here is either
  **six plain coloured paper strips** or **one plain undecorated cloth flag on a stick**, and the
  prompts say so on purpose. Do not soften that wording and do not “improve” it by naming a country.
- **🚨 The map prompts carry “no writing, no place names, no labels.”** The land shape is a plain
  green silhouette; the class learns the name from the printed label, not from the art.
- **Every prompt that shows a person** (five posters, the hello card, the control card and all eight
  action cards) carries *clothed, flat cartoon illustration, children's book art, no photo-realism*:
  the AI Moderator flags people-heavy prompts and this is the wording that clears it. Never give an
  age in years, never describe a body, never mention skin.
- **Every animal is friendly** — ears up, calm, no bared teeth, no hunting, no chase. These are
  matching cards for a two-year-old, not wildlife photography.
- Save each upscale as the exact `.png` filename below; `mj_convert.sh 24` turns them into the
  `.jpg` the page asks for.
