# Midjourney prompts — Circle Time, site Week 20 · “Chinese New Year” (Feb 1–5)

Sheet week 22 in the principal's plan. 37 images → `public/circle-time-images/week20/`.
Runbook: `docs/circle-time/mj-prompts-README.md`. Style is LOCKED — do not paraphrase the suffix.

- **8 posters** `--ar 3:4`, saved 928×1232 · **28 cards + 1 badge** `--ar 1:1`, saved 1000×1000
- Flags on every prompt: `--raw --stylize 50 --v 8.2`
- Submit one prompt per 30 s through the authenticated `midjourney.com/imagine` tab.
- Cards are hyper-literal and singular — one object, centred, nothing else in frame.
- **🚨 Everything red must be BLANK.** Red envelopes, lanterns, couplet strips and the 福 diamond
  carry no characters, no writing and no pattern-that-looks-like-writing — the locked negative list
  forbids text, and MJ renders Chinese characters as nonsense. The prompts below say “completely
  blank” on purpose; do not soften it. The class writes/reads the characters on the printed labels,
  not in the art.
- **Every prompt that shows a person** (five posters, the visit card, the control card and all eight
  action cards) carries *clothed, flat cartoon illustration, children's book art, no photo-realism*:
  the AI Moderator flags people-heavy prompts and this is the wording that clears it. Never give an
  age in years, never describe a body, never mention skin.
- The lion is a **costume** — a colourful dance lion, friendly and silly, never a real big cat.
- Save each upscale as the exact `.png` filename below; `mj_convert.sh 20` turns them into the
  `.jpg` the page asks for.
