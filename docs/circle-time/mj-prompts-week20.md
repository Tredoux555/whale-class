# Midjourney prompts — Circle Time, site Week 18 · “Beijing” (Jan 18–22)

Sheet week 20 in the principal's plan. 37 images → `public/circle-time-images/week18/`.
Runbook: `docs/circle-time/mj-prompts-README.md`. Style is LOCKED — do not paraphrase the suffix.

- **8 posters** `--ar 3:4`, saved 928×1232 · **28 cards + 1 badge** `--ar 1:1`, saved 1000×1000
- Flags on every prompt: `--raw --stylize 50 --v 8.2`
- Submit one prompt per 30 s through the authenticated `midjourney.com/imagine` tab.
- Cards are hyper-literal and singular — one object, centred, nothing else in frame.
- **Every prompt that shows a person** carries *clothed, flat cartoon illustration, children's book art, no photo-realism*: the AI Moderator flags people-heavy prompts and this is the wording that clears it. Never give an age in years, never describe a body, never mention skin.
- **Landmarks stay simple and generic.** Beijing's monuments are drawn as plain shapes a three-year-old can name — a red gate tower, a stone wall with a watchtower, a studded red door, a round woven stadium. Every landmark prompt carries *no flags, no banners, no signs, no emblems, no portraits*: national symbols and lettering are exactly what MJ hallucinates into a Chinese street scene, and a poster carrying one is unusable in a classroom. Do not name a specific building or a person in a prompt.
- The map prompts say *no writing on the map, no place names* for the same reason — the flag the child plants is the label.
- Save each upscale as the exact `.png` filename below; `mj_convert.sh 18` turns them into the `.jpg` the page asks for.
