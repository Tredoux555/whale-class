- Flags on every prompt: `--raw --stylize 50 --v 8.2`
- Submit one prompt per 30 s through the authenticated `midjourney.com/imagine` tab.
- Cards are hyper-literal and singular — one object, centred, nothing else in frame. A three-year-old
  has to be able to name the card from across the mat.
- **The life-cycle stages are single subjects, never a diagram.** The egg, the caterpillar, the
  chrysalis and the butterfly are four separate cards; the egg, the tadpole and the frog are three
  separate cards; the egg, the chick and the hen are three separate cards. Never ask MJ for “a life
  cycle”, an arrow, a numbered sequence or a circle of stages — it renders arrows and numbers, which
  the locked negative list forbids, and the *children* build the sequence out of the printed cards.
  That building is the lesson.
- **Every prompt that shows a person** (seven posters and all eight action cards) carries
  *clothed, flat cartoon illustration, children's book art, no photo-realism*: the AI Moderator flags
  people-heavy prompts and this is the wording that clears it. Never give an age in years, never
  describe a body, never mention skin.
- Animals are friendly and plain — a real chick, a real frog, a real lamb, not a cartoon character
  with a face on it. No hats, no clothes, no props on the animals.
- Save each upscale as the exact `.png` filename below; `mj_convert.sh 25` turns them into the
  `.jpg` the page asks for.
