# Midjourney prompts — Circle Time, site Week 23 · “One Continent — Africa” (Mar 15–19)

Sheet week 25 in the principal's plan. 37 images → `public/circle-time-images/week23/`.
Runbook: `docs/circle-time/mj-prompts-README.md`. Style is LOCKED — do not paraphrase the suffix.

- **8 posters** `--ar 3:4`, saved 928×1232 · **28 cards + 1 badge** `--ar 1:1`, saved 1000×1000
- Flags on every prompt: `--raw --stylize 50 --v 8.2`
- Submit one prompt per 30 s through the authenticated `midjourney.com/imagine` tab.
- Cards are hyper-literal and singular — one object, centred, nothing else in frame.
- **🚨 Every map or continent shape must be BLANK.** No writing, no place names, no country
  borders, no flags, no compass letters — the locked negative list forbids text and MJ renders
  labels as nonsense. The map is a plain coloured shape; the class reads the names off the
  printed labels, not off the art.
- **Every prompt that shows a person** (five posters and all eight action cards) carries
  *clothed, flat cartoon illustration, children's book art, no photo-realism*: the AI Moderator
  flags people-heavy prompts and this is the wording that clears it. Keep children generic —
  everyday clothes, never an age in years, never a body description, never anything about skin,
  hair type or ethnicity, and **no costume, no face paint, no tribal dress, no headdress**.
  These are the Whale Class children on a classroom rug, not a picture of a place's people.
- **Animals are friendly, calm and whole** — a lion with a soft open mouth, never bared fangs,
  never blood, never hunting or fighting. They are three-year-olds' first pictures of these
  animals.
- Nothing in this week is South Africa — no flag, no Table Mountain, no zebra. That is Week 24.
- Save each upscale as the exact `.png` filename below; `mj_convert.sh 23` turns them into the
  `.jpg` the page asks for.
