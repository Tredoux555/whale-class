# Midjourney prompts — Circle Time, site Week 28 · “Landforms” (Apr 19–23)

Sheet week 28 in the principal's plan, re-dated to a **five-day** week by the printed-plan re-plan
(`YEAR_CALENDAR_2026-27.md`). 37 images → `public/circle-time-images/week28/`.
Runbook: `docs/circle-time/mj-prompts-README.md`. Style is LOCKED — do not paraphrase the suffix.

- **8 posters** `--ar 3:4`, saved 928×1232 · **28 cards + 1 badge** `--ar 1:1`, saved 1000×1000
- Flags on every prompt: `--raw --stylize 50 --v 8.2`
- Submit one prompt per 30 s through the authenticated `midjourney.com/imagine` tab.
- Cards are hyper-literal and singular — one object, centred, nothing else in frame.
- **The landform prompts are deliberately plain**: one land form, one shape, no people, no weather, no
  buildings, no animals. A three-year-old has to be able to match the card to the clay model on the
  shelf, and a pretty scene with a cottage in it cannot be matched.
- **Every prompt that shows a person** (the eight action cards, the sentence-frames poster and the
  chorus poster) carries *clothed, flat cartoon illustration, children's book art, no photo-realism*:
  the AI Moderator flags people-heavy prompts and this is the wording that clears it. Never give an
  age in years, never describe a body, never mention skin.
- Keep the island and the lake visually opposite: the island is land ringed by water, the lake is
  water ringed by land. That contrast is the whole week and the art has to carry it.
- Save each upscale as the exact `.png` filename below; `mj_convert.sh 28` turns them into the `.jpg`
  the page asks for.
