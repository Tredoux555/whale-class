Sheet week 38 in the principal's plan — the **last week of the year**. 37 images → `public/circle-time-images/week36/`.
Runbook: `docs/circle-time/mj-prompts-README.md`. Style is LOCKED — do not paraphrase the suffix.

- **8 posters** `--ar 3:4`, saved 928×1232 · **28 cards + 1 badge** `--ar 1:1`, saved 1000×1000
- Flags on every prompt: `--raw --stylize 50 --v 8.2`
- Submit one prompt per 30 s through the authenticated `midjourney.com/imagine` tab.
- Cards are hyper-literal and singular — one object, centred, nothing else in frame. A three-year-old
  has to be able to name the card from across the mat.
- **Every certificate, scroll, card, book, badge and bunting in this week is BLANK.** Graduation art is
  exactly where Midjourney wants to hallucinate lettering — a diploma with fake words on it, a banner
  reading CONGRATULATTONS. Every such prompt says *completely blank with no writing of any kind* **and**
  carries the locked negative list. Reject any upscale with a mark that looks like a letter, however
  pretty the rest of it is.
- **Every prompt that shows a person** (all eight posters, three of the 3-part cards and all eight action
  cards) carries *clothed, flat cartoon illustration, children's book art, no photo-realism*: the AI
  Moderator flags people-heavy prompts and this is the wording that clears it. Never give an age in
  years, never describe a body, never mention skin.
- The graduation caps are **plain** — a black mortarboard and a tassel, nothing printed on them.
- Save each upscale as the exact `.png` filename below; `mj_convert.sh 36` turns them into the
  `.jpg` the page asks for.
