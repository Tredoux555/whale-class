## WEEK 32 · 10–14 May · Big Bang and the Universe
**The one big idea:** it was dark and small — then BANG — and there were stars.
(This is Montessori's First Great Lesson, told at 3-year-old size.)

**Five words they'll own by Friday:** `dark` · `light` · `star` · `big` · `bang`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Littles (2.5–3)** | One word + a gesture is a win: **"Dark!"** (hands over eyes) · **"BANG!"** (clap + jump) · **"Star!"** (twinkle fingers) |
| **Bigs (4–6)** | *"It is dark."* · *"I see a star."* · *"The universe is very big."* |

### Magic Box, day by day
| Day | In the box |
|---|---|
| Mon | **Nothing.** A black cloth over an empty box — "What's in the box?" … *nothing at all* |
| Tue | A **torch** — the first light |
| Wed | A **balloon** — blow it bigger, bigger, BIGGER… **BANG!** |
| Thu | **Glow-in-the-dark stars** (turn the lights off) |
| Fri | All the week's props + black paper and white chalk |

### The 13-minute flow (same every day)
1. **2 min — Magic Box hook.** Box chant → shake → sniff → dramatic peek.
2. **4 min — Teach.** The day's word, dramatised with the object. Teacher gets it wrong once so they can correct you.
3. **3 min — Song.** Chorus → today's new verse → chorus.
4. **3 min — Game.** (Mon: *Lights off / lights on* — hands over eyes, torch on. Tue: *Torch hunt* — shine on a thing, they name it. Wed: *Balloon countdown* — grow small→big→BANG. Thu: *Star freeze* — dance, freeze, twinkle. Fri: prop quick-fire review.)
5. **1 min — Close.** Whisper → louder → loudest: **"Dark… dark… BANG! STARS!"**

### Song · "The Big Bang Boom" (ukulele, C · F · G7)
**Chorus — every single day**
```
C                        F              C
Dark! Dark! Everywhere dark!   (hands over eyes)
F               C          G7        C
Then — a big, big, BIG BANG!   (clap and JUMP on BANG)
C                        F              C
Stars! Stars! Look at the stars!   (twinkle fingers up high)
F          C        G7      C
The universe goes BANG!
```
**One new verse a day (verses use only C and G7)**
- **Mon · Dark** — `C` It was dark, dark, dark — no light at all, / `G7` No sun, no moon, `C` no bouncy ball!
- **Tue · Light** — `C` Then came the light — bright, bright, bright! / `G7` I can see! I can see! `C` What a lovely light!
- **Wed · Bang** — `C` Big! Big! Bigger! Then… BANG! / `G7` Everything went `C` bang-bang-BANG!
- **Thu · Stars** — `C` Little stars, little stars, twinkling up high, / `G7` Millions of stars `C` in the great big sky!
- **Fri · Universe** — `C` Dark and light and stars and me — / `G7` The universe is `C` BIG as big can be!

**Friday:** chorus → all five verses → chorus. That run-through *is* the week's review.

### Theme shelf · four trays (present Monday, free choice all week)
1. **Light / Dark sorting** — a black tray and a white tray + picture cards (torch, sun, star, candle / cave, night, black cat, closed box). *Control: a colour dot on the back of each card.*
2. **Star size grading** — 5 felt stars, biggest → smallest. *Control: line the points up and the row steps evenly.*
3. **Torch explore box** — a shoebox with a hole, pictures inside. Shine the torch, say **"I see a ___."** *Control: matching picture cards outside the box.*
4. **Universe 3-part cards** — sun / moon / star / planet / rocket. *Control: whole control cards.*

### Friday parent wrap-up
This week we told the biggest story of all: it was dark and tiny, then **BANG** — and stars.
Words: *dark, light, star, big, bang.* Ask them to sing the chorus — they jump on **BANG!**
Dark Phonics: **-ng** ("bang, ring, sing").

## WEEK 33 · 17–21 May · Solar System
**The one big idea:** the sun is hot and everything goes round and round it — including us.

**Five words:** `sun` · `moon` · `Earth` · `round` · `hot`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Littles** | **"Sun!"** (draw a circle with both arms) · **"Hot!"** (fan your face) · **"Round!"** (spin) |
| **Bigs** | *"The sun is hot."* · *"The moon is round."* · *"Earth is my home."* |

### Magic Box, day by day
| Day | In the box |
|---|---|
| Mon | Torch + a **yellow ball** — the sun |
| Tue | A **globe** (or a blue-green ball) — our home |
| Wed | A **white ball poked full of little craters** — the moon |
| Thu | **Fruit planets** — peppercorn, grape, apple, orange, grapefruit, watermelon |
| Fri | All of it, laid out on a black cloth |

### Flow
Same 13-minute shape. Games: Mon *hot/cold* (warm bottle vs ice — "Hot!" / "Cold!"). Tue *pass the Earth*. Wed *day and night* (lights on = day, torch = night). Thu *planet parade* — line the fruit up by size. Fri *walk the orbit* — hold hands, walk one lap round the "sun" while singing.

### Song · "Round and Round" (C · F · G7 · Am)
**Chorus**
```
C                    F           C
Round and round and round we go!   (spin on the spot)
F            C        G7        C
Round the sun — go slow, go slow!  (whole class walks one circle)
C                 F            C
The sun is hot, the moon is cold,  (fan yourself / shiver)
F           C        G7      C
And Earth is my home, I am told!   (hug yourself)
```
- **Mon · Sun** — `C` The sun is hot! The sun is bright! / `G7` It keeps me warm `C` and gives me light!
- **Tue · Earth** — `C` Earth, Earth, my home is Earth — / `G7` Blue and green, `C` the best on Earth!
- **Wed · Moon** — `C` The moon is white, the moon is round, / `G7` It comes at night `C` without a sound!
- **Thu · Planets** — `C` Eight round planets in a row, / `G7` Round and round the sun `C` they go, go, GO!
- **Fri · Round** — `C` Round and round and round and round, / `G7` Everybody `C` spin around!

### Theme shelf · four trays
1. **Solar system orbit mat** — a black mat with painted rings, a sun card, 8 planet balls placed near → far. *Control: numbered dots under each ring.*
2. **Solar system 3-part cards** — sun / Earth / moon / planet / orbit.
3. **Hot & cold sensorial pairs** — warm water bottle / ice pack + hot-things and cold-things cards. *Control: red and blue dots on the card backs.*
4. **Moon phase ordering** — 4 discs (new · crescent · half · full) laid against a control strip. *Control: the strip.*

### Friday parent wrap-up
We learned the sun is hot, the moon is round, and Earth is our home. We made a solar system out of fruit and walked one whole orbit round the sun.
Dark Phonics: **sh** ("shine, ship, shadow").

## WEEK 34 · 24–28 May · Space Exploration
**The one big idea:** people build rockets and *go there* — and Chinese people go too (嫦娥 / our astronauts).

**Five words:** `rocket` · `astronaut` · `up` · `down` · `blast off`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Littles** | **"Up!"** (arms high) · **"Down!"** (crouch) · **"BLAST OFF!"** (jump) |
| **Bigs** | *"I am an astronaut."* · *"My rocket goes up."* · *"Five, four, three, two, one — blast off!"* |

### Magic Box, day by day
| Day | In the box |
|---|---|
| Mon | A **cardboard-tube rocket** |
| Tue | A **helmet** made from a box, big enough to actually wear |
| Wed | A **moon rock** — a stone wrapped in foil |
| Thu | A **little flag** — to plant on the moon |
| Fri | Everything + the 5-4-3-2-1 number cards |

### Flow
Same shape. Games: Mon *rocket launch* (crouch, countdown, JUMP). Tue *dress the astronaut* (one child, real sequence: helmet, suit, gloves, boots). Wed *moon walk* — bouncy slow-motion walking. Thu *plant the flag* — an obstacle path to the "moon". Fri *countdown relay* + prop review.

### Song · "Blast Off!" (C · F · G7)
**Chorus**
```
C              F            C
Five, four, three, two, ONE —   (crouch lower on each number)
F        C       G7       C
BLAST OFF! Here we come!        (everyone JUMPS)
C                F              C
Up, up, up to the moon we fly,  (arms up, sway)
F           C        G7      C
Wave to the Earth — good-bye!   (wave)
```
- **Mon · Rocket** — `C` My rocket is red, my rocket is tall, / `G7` Up, up, up — `C` I'm not going to fall!
- **Tue · Astronaut** — `C` Helmet on! Big boots too! / `G7` I'm an astronaut — `C` how about you?
- **Wed · Up & down** — `C` Up, up, up — we go so high! / `G7` Down, down, down — `C` good-bye, good-bye!
- **Thu · Moon walk** — `C` Bouncy, bouncy on the moon, / `G7` Slow, slow, slow — `C` like a big balloon!
- **Fri · Countdown** — `C` Five, four, three, two, one — / `G7` Blast off, `C` everyone!

### Theme shelf · four trays
1. **Countdown matching** — numerals 5–1 + rocket quantity cards. *Control: count the rockets.*
2. **Astronaut dressing sequence** — 4 cards (helmet → suit → gloves → boots) + the Montessori dressing frames beside it. *Control: numbers on the card backs.*
3. **Space 3-part cards** — rocket / astronaut / moon / star / flag.
4. **Moon surface tray** — a flour-and-sand tray; drop marbles to make craters, then tweezer the "moon rocks" into a bowl. *Control: crater picture card; the bowl holds exactly 5.*

### Friday parent wrap-up
We built rockets, wore a helmet, walked on the moon in slow motion and planted a flag. We counted backwards from five every single day.
Dark Phonics: **bl-** ("blast off, blue, black").

## WEEK 35 · 31 May – 4 June · Dinosaurs and Fossils (1)
**The one big idea:** long, long ago the biggest animals ever walked right here. *(六一儿童节 falls on Tuesday 1 June.)*

**Five words:** `dinosaur` · `big` · `teeth` · `roar` · `egg`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Littles** | **"ROAR!"** (big open mouth) · **"Big!"** (arms wide) · **"Egg!"** (hands make a ball) |
| **Bigs** | *"It is a big dinosaur."* · *"It has sharp teeth."* · *"I can roar!"* |

### Magic Box, day by day
| Day | In the box |
|---|---|
| Mon | A **big dinosaur model** |
| Tue | A **giant paper tooth** — then a mirror to find their own little ones |
| Wed | A **dinosaur egg** buried in sand |
| Thu | A **huge paper footprint** — everyone stands inside it |
| Fri | All the props + a long string for measuring |

### Flow
Same shape. Games: Mon *big / small* (stomp like a big dinosaur, tiptoe like a tiny one). Tue *count the teeth*. Wed *crack the egg* — guess what's inside. Thu *roar loud, roar quiet* — volume control game. Fri *measure a dinosaur* — roll the string down the corridor, whole class walks its length.

### Song · "Dinosaur Stomp" (C · F · G7 · Am)
**Chorus**
```
C              F           C
Stomp, stomp, stomp — big feet!   (stomp on every beat)
F         C       G7        C
ROAR! ROAR! Show your teeth!      (roar, bare teeth)
C                  F              C
Long, long neck and a swishy tail, (arm neck, tail swish)
F            C        G7       C
Dinosaur, dinosaur — big as a whale!
```
- **Mon · Dinosaur** — `C` Dinosaur, dinosaur, big and tall, / `G7` Long, long ago — `C` the biggest of all!
- **Tue · Teeth** — `C` Big sharp teeth — one, two, three, / `G7` This dinosaur is `C` smiling at me!
- **Wed · Egg** — `C` In the nest there is an egg, / `G7` Crack, crack, crack — `C` a tiny leg!
- **Thu · Roar** — `C` I can roar! I can ROAR! / `G7` Louder, louder — `C` roar some more!
- **Fri · Big & small** — `C` Big, big dinosaur, small, small me — / `G7` Stomp together, `C` one, two, three!

### Theme shelf · four trays
1. **Dinosaur size grading** — 5 dinosaurs, biggest → smallest. *Control: shadow outlines printed on the mat.*
2. **Dinosaur 3-part cards** — T-rex / triceratops / stegosaurus / egg / tooth.
3. **Footprint matching** — footprint cards ↔ dinosaur cards. *Control: matching symbols on the backs.*
4. **Dig the dinosaur eggs** — sand tray, spoon, brush, tongs; 5 eggs into 5 nest hollows. *Control: 5 hollows, 5 eggs.* (Practical life — fine motor.)

### Friday parent wrap-up
Dinosaurs! We roared, stomped, counted giant teeth, dug eggs out of the sand and measured a dinosaur down the corridor. Happy 六一!
Dark Phonics: **th** ("teeth, the, this").

## WEEK 36 · 7–11 June · Dinosaurs and Fossils (2) + May review
**The one big idea:** the dinosaurs left something behind in the rock — and we can dig it up.

**Five words:** `fossil` · `bone` · `dig` · `rock` · `old`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Littles** | **"Dig!"** (digging action) · **"Bone!"** (hold it up) · **"Old!"** (stroke a long beard) |
| **Bigs** | *"I can dig."* · *"This rock is very old."* · *"I found a fossil!"* |

### Magic Box, day by day
| Day | In the box |
|---|---|
| Mon | A **real fossil** — or a shell pressed into clay |
| Tue | A **bone** (model, or a scrubbed clean one) |
| Wed | A **small brush and trowel** — the palaeontologist's tools |
| Thu | **The clay fossil they made on Wednesday**, now dry |
| Fri | The whole month's props — stars, planets, rocket, dinosaur, fossil |

### Flow
Same shape. Games: Mon *what's under the sand?* Tue *build the skeleton* (bones onto an outline). Wed *brush it gently* — slow, careful, quiet. Thu *old or new?* (a fossil vs a plastic toy). Fri **May finale** — hold up each prop from all five weeks and let them shout the word.

### Song · "Dig, Dig, Dig" (C · F · G7)
**Chorus**
```
C            F         C
Dig, dig, dig in the sand,   (digging action)
F        C      G7       C
What is in my hand?          (open both palms)
C            F           C
A bone! A shell! A rock so old! (hold each one up)
F         C        G7     C
A fossil — worth more than gold!
```
- **Mon · Fossil** — `C` A fossil, a fossil — a stone that's old, / `G7` A dinosaur story `C` waiting to be told!
- **Tue · Bone** — `C` Bones, bones — one, two, three, / `G7` Put them back — `C` a dinosaur for me!
- **Wed · Dig** — `C` Dig with a spoon, brush with a brush, / `G7` Slowly, slowly — `C` no big rush!
- **Thu · Old** — `C` Old, old, old — so long ago, / `G7` Before you, before me — `C` a long time ago!
- **Fri · May finale** — `C` Stars and planets, rockets too, / `G7` Dinosaurs and fossils — `C` I love you!

### Theme shelf · four trays
1. **Make a fossil** — clay discs + shells, leaves, a dinosaur foot. Press, lift, dry. *Control: the print matches the object beside it.*
2. **Excavation tray** — a sand block, brush and tweezers; uncover the bones and lay them on the skeleton outline card. *Control: the outline card.*
3. **Fossil 3-part cards** — fossil / bone / shell / rock / dig.
4. **Old → new timeline strip** — 4 pictures: dinosaur → fossil in rock → museum skeleton → a bird today. *Control: numbers on the backs.* (This is where dinosaurs quietly turn into birds.)

### Friday parent + month wrap-up
Five weeks in space and deep time: it went **BANG**, the stars appeared, the planets started spinning, people built rockets and flew to the moon — and long before any of that, dinosaurs stomped around here and left their bones in the rock. We dug them up.
Dark Phonics: **ch** ("chomp, chip, munch").

## Weekly rituals (unchanged, every week)
- **Attention grabber:** "One, two, three — eyes on me!" → "One, two — eyes on you!"
- **The Magic Box:** same box, same chant, same shake-sniff-peek. The week's prop lives in it.
- **The two-tier rule:** littles = one word + gesture. Bigs = a sentence frame. Never make a little produce a sentence.
- **Whisper → shout close:** every day ends with the theme line, quiet to loud.
- **Teacher fails on purpose:** get one thing wrong a day so they correct you.
- **One song a week:** same chorus daily, one new verse each morning, whole song Friday. Friday's finale *is* the review.
