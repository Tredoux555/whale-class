# Week 2 · "My Body" — Midjourney prompt pack

**38 prompts**, mirroring the week-1 set exactly: 8 posters (+1 alternate theme poster,
as week 1 kept one) · 6 three-part body cards · 2 sorting signs · 12 sorting cards ·
8 action cards · 1 award badge.

**Target folder:** `public/circle-time-images/week2/`
**Filename convention:** `ct-week2-<slug>.png` from Midjourney → convert to
`ct-week2-<slug>.jpg` for the repo (same as week 1).

**Style rules carried over from week 1 (these are the ones that worked):**
- Style phrase **"soft gouache storybook"** goes immediately after the subject, not at the end.
- Cards open with `one single …` or `a pair of simple friendly cartoon …` — isolated object,
  no scene, no props, no hands holding it.
- Character cards open with `a <ethnicity> preschool boy/girl <doing the action>`; ethnicity
  is varied deliberately across the set.
- Plain cream or plain white background, always. No border, no frame, no shadow scene.
- Keep prompts **short and literal**. The earlier learning holds: over-embellished prompts
  failed; hyper-literal, negative-constrained prompts worked.
- The negative clause is included verbatim on every single prompt — MJ hallucinates stray
  text into children's illustrations.

**Flags:** posters `--ar 3:4 --v 8.2` · cards and the badge `--ar 1:1 --v 8.2 --raw --stylize 50`
· every prompt ends `--no text, letters, words, watermark`.
