# How to Use the Two Phonics Systems
*The super simple guide. One page. That's it.*

---

## The one idea

You built **two doors into the same phonics path** (sounds in the same order, s → a → t → p …):

1. **The Weekly Curriculum** (Curriculum Studio) — your *week-by-week teaching plan*. One sound per week, everything printed for you.
2. **Dark Phonics** — the *fun library*: one silly song per sound, plus flashcards and little books.

Studio tells you what to do this week. Dark Phonics makes it catchy.

---

## Your week (5 minutes of prep)

1. Go to **montree.xyz → Library → Curriculum Studio**.
2. Open **this week's card** (Week 1 = a, Week 2 = t, … it's in order — just do the next one).
3. **Play the videos** — the Dark Phonics song plays first, then the week's songs. Play them every day. Repetition is the whole trick.
4. **Print the pack** (one click). Print card PDFs **two-sided with normal settings** — fronts and backs line up by themselves.
5. Use a little each day: flash the cards, one worksheet, sing again.

That's the entire system. Next week, open the next week.

---

## Dark Phonics (the library)

**montree.xyz → Library → Dark Phonics** (first card). Five tabs:

- **🎵 Songs** — one song per sound, lesson 5–53. Play, sing, download.
- **🃏 Flashcards** — per song: the **Letter Card** (letter front / sound back) and the **Vocab Cards** (the scene — snake IN the sock — then each word: picture front / word back). Plus one big all-lessons deck.
- **📕 Picture Books & 📗 Easy Readers** — little books the child reads ALONE. Each reader says **"Gate: Lesson N"** = hand it out once the class has passed that song. (No books early on — you can't write a book with one sound.)
- **🎤 Sing-Along Cards** — lyric cards for circle time.

*(The 🛠 Workshop corner is only for making new content — teachers never need it.)*

---

## The daily rhythm (10 minutes)

1. **Sing** the song (the class will demand it anyway).
2. **Flash** the cards — scene card, then the words.
3. **One printed activity** from the week's pack.
4. When a reader's gate is passed → the child **reads the book alone**. That's the payoff moment.

---

## If you forget everything else

Open Curriculum Studio. Do the next week. Play the songs daily. Print two-sided. Hand out books at their gate. Done.
