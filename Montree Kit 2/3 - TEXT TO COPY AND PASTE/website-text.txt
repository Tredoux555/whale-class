MONTREE
The AI Montessori classroom revolution

Montree was built by a Montessori teacher, for one reason:
to help the teacher.

A teacher takes a photo of a child working.
Montree does the rest.

It knows which Montessori work is in the photo. It writes
the observation. It tracks the child's progress. And every
week, it writes a real, personal report for the parents -
not a template, a real report about their child.

Teachers get their time back. Parents feel closer to the
classroom than ever before. Principals can see the whole
school at a glance.

It is the kind of thing you have to use to understand.
