SHORT TEXT ABOUT MONTREE
=========================
Use this anywhere you need one short paragraph -
for example under our logo on your partners page.
----------------------------------------------------------------


Montree is a new tool for Montessori classrooms, built by
a Montessori teacher. A teacher takes a photo of a child
working, and Montree records the observation, tracks the
child's progress, and writes weekly reports for parents.
Learn more at montree.xyz
