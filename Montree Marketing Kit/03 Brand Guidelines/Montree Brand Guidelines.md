# Montree Brand Guidelines

## The logo

The official Montree mark is a **gold serif "M" on deep forest green** (#03261D). It is the only current logo — an earlier teal "sprout" mark is retired and must not be used.

Three variants, all included in this kit:

1. **Full logo (green field)** — the M on its deep green square. Default choice. Works anywhere.
2. **Transparent mark** — the gold M alone, transparent background. Use when placing the logo over your own background. Best on dark or muted backgrounds; avoid busy imagery behind it.
3. **Tile** — square app-icon style. For grids, directories, and "our partners" strips.

### Rules

- Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, outline, or add effects to the M.
- Do not recreate the logo in another font — always use the supplied files.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least 25% of its width.
- Minimum display size: 32 px. Below that, use the supplied favicon-scale icons on request.
- On light/white backgrounds, prefer the full logo (green field) so the gold keeps its contrast.

- **Headings:** Lora (serif) — free on Google Fonts.
- **Body / UI:** Inter — free on Google Fonts.

## Look and feel

Calm and natural. Earthy, organic, premium. Generous dark space with emerald and gold accents on deep forest green. Imagery that fits the brand: warm classroom light, small hands at work, wooden Montessori materials, nature.

## Voice

Warm, practical, educator-to-educator. Never salesy. Montree was built by a working Montessori teacher, and the writing should sound like one: plain, confident, and short. When in doubt, say less and link to montree.xyz.
