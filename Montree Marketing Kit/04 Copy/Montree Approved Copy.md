## Founder story (longer, for features/interviews)

Montree wasn't built by a software company. It was built by a Montessori teacher who spent his evenings writing observation records and parent reports, and realised the technology finally existed to hand that work to a machine — without losing what makes Montessori observation meaningful. Every feature was tested the next morning in his own classroom in Beijing. What started as one teacher's tool is now a full platform: photo-based observation, curriculum tracking across all five areas, weekly parent reports, and a built-in Montessori expert for principals and parents. The goal never changed: give teachers their time back so they can return to the children, the classroom, and the craft.
