MONTREE - MARKETING KIT
========================

Thank you for helping us share Montree!

This kit has 3 folders. Here is what to do with each one:


FOLDER 1 - LOGO
----------------
Two logo files:

  montree-logo.png
  This is the main logo. A gold "M" on a dark green square.
  Use this one on your website and anywhere else.

  montree-logo-no-background.png
  This is the gold "M" with no background.
  Use this one if you want to put the logo on top of
  your own colours or pictures.

Please do not change the colours of the logo,
and do not stretch it. Just use it as it is.


FOLDER 2 - PICTURES FOR FACEBOOK AND SOCIAL MEDIA
--------------------------------------------------
  profile-picture.png  -  use as a profile picture
  cover-photo.png      -  use as a Facebook or page cover photo
  story-post.png       -  use for a WhatsApp status,
                          Facebook story, or Instagram story


FOLDER 3 - TEXT TO COPY AND PASTE
----------------------------------
Ready-made text about Montree. Just copy and paste it.
There is text for your website, for a Facebook post,
and for a WhatsApp message.

Please use this text as it is written. Montree is hard
to explain in your own words - this text works.

Always use this website link, typed exactly like this:

  montree.xyz
